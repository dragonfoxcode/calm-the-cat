# Calm the Cat

A classroom noise timer, made as a cat version of
[Grow a Dragon](https://github.com/dragonfoxcode/grow-a-dragon). It works like a
chess clock for loud and quiet moments, and can switch between the two on its own
by listening to the room.

The class cat purrs and calms down during quiet time until she falls asleep.
Noise makes her crazier and crazier, up to a full CAT-astrophe that scratches
and swats at the screen.

- **Sweet dreams:** while she sleeps through quiet time she has sweet dreams.
  Each one unlocks something new in Cat Style: coats, collars, hats, glasses
  and bed colors.
- **Cat Gallery:** adopt several cats, each with her own name and look, and
  pick which one comes to class.
- **Scenes:** pick a scene for her room (Cozy Night, Sunny Morning, Rainy Day,
  Snow Day Cabin, City Loft, Northern Lights, Submarine, Space Cat), or open
  the Scene Remixer and mix your own window view, wallpaper, picture, floor,
  rug and extras. Remix! picks a surprise.
- **Care breaks:** every few dreams she wakes up needing a drink, a snack, the
  litter box or a little play. Leave the litter box unscooped and the poop
  piles up until she gets upset.

## Use it

**Play it online:** https://dragonfoxcode.github.io/calm-the-cat/

Or download `calm-the-cat.html` and open it in a web browser. Either way it's
one file, and progress is saved in that browser.

Calm the Cat is built on the [Classroom Timer Template](https://github.com/dragonfoxcode/classroom-timer-template).
